    \chapter*{Chapter 1: The Perfect Life}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Chapter 1: The Perfect Life}
\textit{Emma's daily routine, showcasing her successful psychology practice and loving relationship with Liam.}

The sunlight streaming through the kitchen window highlighted the warmth
of the Taylor residence, casting a golden glow on the meticulously
arranged breakfast spread before Emma. She carefully placed a sprinkle
of fresh berries atop the fluffy omelette, stepping back to admire her
handiwork. The aroma of freshly brewed coffee wafted through the air,
mingling with the sweet scent of blooming flowers outside, creating an
inviting ambiance that signaled the start of another serene day.

"Liam will love this," Emma murmured to herself, a gentle smile playing
on her lips as she envisioned his reaction. She had always taken pride
in starting their days off right, and today was no exception. The soft
hum of the refrigerator provided a soothing background melody as she
poured two glasses of freshly squeezed orange juice.

Just then, the sound of footsteps echoed from the hallway, growing
louder with each passing moment. Emma's smile broadened in anticipation.
"Good morning, beautiful," Liam's deep, melodious voice filled the room,
accompanied by the soft rustle of his suit jacket as he entered the
kitchen.

Emma turned, her expressive green eyes sparkling with warmth. "Good
morning, handsome. I made your favorite," she said, gesturing towards
the breakfast spread. Liam's piercing blue eyes crinkled at the corners
as he smiled, his captivating smile illuminating the space between them.

The next few moments were a harmonious dance of shared laughter and
gentle conversation, each savoring not just the meal but the warmth of
their morning ritual together. As they finished up, Emma glanced at her
watch, a subtle hint of restlessness creeping into her otherwise serene
demeanor. "I should get going. I have an early session today."

Liam nodded, his expression supportive. "You'll do great, as always. I
might just surprise you with lunch at your practice later." The promise
hung in the air like a sweet whisper, leaving Emma with a sense of
cherished anticipation as she gathered her belongings and headed out
into the crisp morning air.

The drive to her psychology practice was a short, pleasant one, the city
awakening around her like a canvas of endless possibilities. Upon
arrival, Emma's professionalism seamlessly took over, her introspective
nature focusing on the day ahead. Her first client, Mrs. Jenkins, was
already seated in the waiting area, flipping through a magazine with an
air of nervous expectancy.

Emma greeted her warmly and led her to the cozy therapy room, where the
soft, natural light pouring in seemed to envelop them in a sense of
comfort and trust. The session that followed was a testament to Emma's
empathetic listening skills and insightful guidance, culminating in a
breakthrough moment for Mrs. Jenkins. As their time drew to a close,
Emma felt a deep sense of fulfillment, her passion for helping others
glowing brightly within her.

The morning sun had now given way to the gentle warmth of afternoon,
casting long shadows across her office as Emma wrapped up her notes from
the session. Just as she was about to delve into her lunch, a knock at
the door interrupted her, followed by the appearance of Liam, a charming
smile on his face and a bouquet of her favorite flowers in hand.

"Surprise," he said, his voice low and playful, as he set down a gourmet
lunch for two. Emma's heart swelled with affection, the gesture touching
the part of her that cherished these thoughtful moments they shared.
Yet, beneath the surface of this idyllic scene, a faint ripple of
restlessness stirred within Emma, a whisper of unmet desires and
unspoken questions, waiting patiently to surface.

As they sat down to enjoy their lunch together, the tension between them
was palpable, yet expertly masked by the ease of their conversation.
Emma couldn't help but feel a twinge of gratitude towards Liam for this
thoughtful gesture, even as her mind wandered to the upcoming sessions
and the weight of her clients' troubles that she carried with her.

Liam, ever the observant one, noticed the fleeting shadow across her
face and reached out, his hand covering hers in a gentle, reassuring
touch. "Everything alright, my love? You seemed a bit preoccupied during
our morning conversation," he asked, his voice laced with concern, yet
subtly probing.

Emma's instincts kicked in, and she swiftly navigated the inquiry with a
practiced smile, one that conveyed appreciation for his thoughtfulness
while adeptly sidestepping the true nature of her thoughts. "Just the
usual, getting into the zone for my next session. Mrs. Jenkins had a
breakthrough today, so I'm feeling quite fulfilled," she replied, her
tone light and engaging.

As they delved into the intricacies of her work, Liam listened intently,
his responses thoughtful and supportive. However, Emma couldn't shake
off the feeling that he was watching her more closely than usual, as if
searching for something beneath the surface of their conversation. She
pushed the thought aside, attributing it to her own paranoia, a
byproduct of dealing with the complexities of human emotions on a daily
basis.

The remainder of their lunch together was filled with laughter and
discussions about everything from their upcoming social engagements to
Liam's latest business ventures. Yet, for Emma, the afternoon sun
casting its warm glow over them seemed to highlight the faint outlines
of a facade, one that she had grown so accustomed to maintaining that it
had become second nature.

As Liam prepared to leave, he leaned in, his lips brushing against her
cheek in a gentle kiss. "I have a meeting this evening, but I promise,
tonight will be all about us. Maybe we can try out that new restaurant
you've been wanting to visit?" The promise hung in the air, a
tantalizing prospect that normally would have filled Emma with
excitement.

Instead, she found herself responding on autopilot, her smile and nod of
agreement masking the growing sense of disconnection within her. It
wasn't until Liam had left, and the office was once again silent, that
Emma allowed herself to confront the whisper that had been growing
louder all day-\/-a whisper that questioned the perfection of her life,
and the true cost of maintaining its flawless facade.

With a deep breath, Emma stood up, her eyes drifting towards the window
where the city pulsed with life outside. She felt an overwhelming urge
to step out into that vibrant tapestry, to lose herself in its rhythms
and perhaps find a piece of herself that she hadn't known was missing.
But for now, she had sessions to prepare for, lives to touch, and a
facade to uphold.

The restlessness within her simmered just below the surface, waiting for
the perfect moment to resurface, as Emma delved back into her work, the
soft click of her pen on paper echoing through the silence-\/-a reminder
that even in the most serene of lives, storms can brew beneath the
surface, waiting to emerge.

As the afternoon wore on, Emma found herself navigating the complexities
of her clients' emotional landscapes with ease, her professional
demeanor a beacon of comfort and understanding. Yet, with each passing
session, the whisper within her grew louder, its presence now a
constant, gnawing companion.

During a brief interlude between sessions, Emma's gaze wandered to the
small, antique music box on her desk, a gift from Liam on their first
anniversary. The delicate, golden ballerina twirling to the soft,
melancholic tune seemed to echo the turmoil brewing inside her. She felt
an inexplicable urge to wind it up, to let its soothing melody calm the
rising tide of her emotions.

Just as her fingers were about to make contact with the box, a knock at
the door broke the spell, heralding the arrival of her next client.
Emma's mask slipped back into place, and she welcomed the young woman,
Rachel, with a warm, empathetic smile.

The session that followed was intense, with Rachel grappling to come to
terms with a recent betrayal by a trusted friend. Emma listened
intently, her responses tailored to guide Rachel through the labyrinth
of her emotions. As the session drew to a close, Emma felt a deep sense
of satisfaction, knowing she had helped Rachel take the first steps
towards healing.

However, as she escorted Rachel out and returned to her office, the
silence that enveloped her seemed oppressive, punctuated only by the
soft tick of the clock on the wall. The music box, once again, caught
her eye, its ballerina frozen in mid-pirouette, a poignant reminder of
the serenity she outwardly embodied, yet inwardly lacked.

With a sense of trepidation, Emma reached for the phone, her fingers
hovering over the keypad as she contemplated calling Sarah, her best
friend and confidante. It had been weeks since they last spoke at
length, and Emma felt an overwhelming need to reconnect, to share with
someone the turmoil simmering beneath her polished exterior.

As she hesitated, a text message from Liam flashed on her screen, its
contents a stark reminder of their evening plans: "Looking forward to
tonight, my love. Shall I pick you up at 7?" The words, once a promise
of a romantic evening, now felt suffocating, the weight of his
expectations pressing down upon her.

With a sense of resignation, Emma typed out a response, her thumbs
moving with a life of their own: "Sounds perfect, darling. See you
then." The phone slipped from her grasp, landing softly on the desk as
she let out a silent sigh, the music box's unplayed melody echoing in
her mind like a lament for the freedom she dared not claim.

The remainder of the day blurred together in a haze of sessions and
paperwork, Emma's actions driven by a sense of obligation rather than
passion. As the sun dipped below the horizon, casting the city in a
warm, golden light, Emma couldn't help but feel trapped, her life a
beautifully crafted prison from which she saw no clear escape.

The sound of her office door closing behind her as she left for the day
was akin to the shutting of a cell door, the finality of it echoing
through her very being. The evening ahead, once anticipated with
excitement, now loomed before her like an uncertain future, its outcome
hanging precariously in the balance.

As Emma stepped into the fading light of day, the city's vibrant pulse
seemed to mock her, its rhythms a stark contrast to the stilted beat of
her own heart. She knew then, with a certainty that both thrilled and
terrified her, that she stood at the precipice of a great change, one
that would either shatter the facade of her perfect life or forever
silence the whispers of her restless soul.

Here is the final chunk of the scene, concluding the narrative
cohesively and meaningfully:

As Emma walked away from her office, the chill of the evening air
whispered against her skin, a stark contrast to the warmth that once
radiated from within. She felt the weight of her phone in her pocket,
Liam's message still glowing on the screen like an unspoken promise. The
city lights began to twinkle around her, a canvas of endless
possibilities, yet Emma's heart remained shrouded in uncertainty.

Her footsteps slowed as she approached the crosswalk, the red hand of
the traffic signal mirroring the pause within her. For a moment, she
closed her eyes, letting the symphony of car horns, chatter, and wailing
sirens wash over her. It was in this fleeting instant, suspended between
the ebb and flow of city life, that Emma realized her choice was not
between perfection and imperfection, but between truth and facade.

The signal turned green, and with it, a spark within Emma ignited. She
didn't step forward immediately; instead, she stood there, the world
moving around her, as she whispered a silent vow to herself: to confront
the whispers, to shatter the illusions, and to brave the uncertainty
that lay beneath the surface of her perfect life.

With a deep breath, Emma opened her eyes, the city's vibrant tapestry
unfolding before her once more. This time, however, she saw it not as a
backdrop for her meticulously crafted existence but as a kaleidoscope of
possibilities, each one waiting to be explored, to be lived. And with
that realization, Emma stepped forward, into the flow of traffic, into
the unknown, and towards the first dawn of her true self.

The city swallowed her whole, its lights and sounds enveloping her like
a promise of tomorrow. In that moment, Emma knew she would never again
confuse the beauty of a facade with the beauty of a life truly lived.
The perfect life, once her ultimate goal, had become merely the first
chapter in the story of her awakening. And as the night wrapped its arms
around her, Emma smiled, knowing that beneath the surface of every
ending lies the promise of a new beginning.

\chapter*{Chapter 2: Cracks in the Facade}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Chapter 2: Cracks in the Facade}
\textit{Subtle hints at Emma's growing desperation and the controlling nature of her husband, Liam.}

The evening shadows cast by the dimly lit lamps in the Taylor residence
seemed to grow longer and darker as night descended upon the suburban
neighborhood. Emma, settled into her favorite armchair, was engrossed in
the latest psychological journal, her mind absorbing the intricacies of
human behavior with the intensity that had always defined her
profession. The soft hum of the air conditioner provided a soothing
background melody, a stark contrast to the sudden, jarring ring of the
phone.

Emma's gaze flickered towards the clock on the mantle before she rose to
answer it, a hint of curiosity mingling with the faintest whisper of
apprehension. "Hello?" Her voice was warm and inviting, yet cautious, as
if sensing the caller's intent before they spoke.

The response was silence. Not the dead, empty kind, but a palpable,
heavy silence that seemed to press against her eardrum. Emma's grip on
the receiver tightened, her heart rate subtly increasing. "Hello? Is
anyone there?" she repeated, her tone now tinged with a slight edge of
concern.

The line crackled to life, a low, indistinguishable murmur escaping
before it was abruptly severed, leaving Emma standing in stunned
silence, the phone still clutched in her hand. She felt a shiver run
down her spine as she slowly returned the receiver to its cradle, her
mind racing with possibilities.

"Liam, I had the strangest phone call just now," Emma began, turning
towards the kitchen where Liam was busy preparing dinner, the savory
aromas of roasted chicken and steaming vegetables wafting through the
air. However, before she could elaborate, Liam's voice cut through, his
tone light yet laced with an undercurrent of inquiry.

"How was your day, dear? Any interesting sessions?" His eyes met hers,
piercing blue and intensely focused, as he paused in his cooking, a
spatula hovering over the sizzling pan.

Emma felt a flicker of unease at the specificity of his question,
particularly the emphasis on her interactions with male clients. It
wasn't the first time Liam had shown interest in this aspect of her
work, but tonight, it seemed to carry a weight that made her skin
prickle with discomfort.

"It was...enlightening," Emma replied, choosing her words with care,
attempting to sidestep the unspoken undercurrents. "Mrs. Jenkins made
significant progress, and I've taken on a new client who presents an
intriguing challenge."

Liam's expression remained neutral, yet his gaze lingered, as if probing
for more. "That sounds fascinating. Perhaps you can tell me more about
it over dinner?" The suggestion was posed casually, but Emma detected
the faintest hint of command beneath the surface.

As they sat down to eat, Liam dominated the conversation, outlining
future plans with a precision that left little room for discussion or
dissent. Emma listened, her smile and nods of agreement masking a
growing sense of suffocation, the earlier phone call now relegated to
the back of her mind but not forgotten.

The meal was consumed in a comfortable silence, the only sound the
clinking of utensils against plates. Yet, beneath the serene surface of
their domestic scene, Emma felt the first hairline cracks appearing in
the facade of her perfect life, threatening to expose the underlying
tensions and unspoken fears that had been simmering for so long.

As the evening wore on, the shadows in the room seemed to deepen,
mirroring the growing unease within Emma. She pushed the thoughts aside,
focusing on the warmth of the dinner table, the soft glow of the
candles, and Liam's charming smile. Yet, with each passing moment, the
silence between them felt more oppressive, a palpable force that pressed
against her chest.

"Liam, may I ask something?" Emma's voice broke the stillness, her tone
light yet laced with a hint of curiosity. "Who was on the phone earlier?
The one who didn't speak?"

Liam's expression remained serene, but his eyes narrowed ever so
slightly, a fleeting glimpse of something more complex beneath the
surface. "I'm not aware of any calls, dear. Must have been a wrong
number." His response was smooth, practiced, yet Emma detected the
faintest whisper of evasion.

She let it slide, attributing her misgivings to the earlier unsettling
phone call. As they finished dinner and began clearing the table, Emma's
thoughts drifted back to her clients, particularly the new one who had
sparked Liam's interest. She made a mental note to review the case files
again, ensuring she was adequately prepared for their next session.

The rest of the evening unfolded with a sense of routine comfort:
loading the dishwasher, watching a light-hearted comedy show, and
eventually retiring to their bedroom. Emma changed into her favorite
silk pajamas, feeling the soft fabric envelop her like a soothing balm.
As she slipped into bed, Liam wrapped his arms around her, pulling her
close.

"Everything alright, my love?" he whispered, his breath warm against her
ear. "You seemed a bit...distracted tonight."

Emma's instincts urged her to confide in him, to share the nagging
doubts and fears that had been accumulating. But something held her back
- perhaps the memory of the unspoken phone call, or the faint sense of
suffocation she'd felt during dinner. Instead, she opted for
reassurance, her voice barely above a whisper.

"I'm fine, just a long day. I appreciate your concern, Liam." Her
response was met with a gentle squeeze, Liam's body relaxing beside hers
as they drifted off to sleep.

In the darkness, Emma's mind began to wander, her thoughts weaving
together the disparate threads of her day: the mysterious phone call,
Liam's probing questions, and the growing sense of disconnection within
their relationship. She felt like she was standing at the edge of a
precipice, staring into an abyss of unknowns, with only the faintest
glimmer of moonlight to guide her.

As sleep finally claimed her, Emma's subconscious whispered a haunting
question, one that would linger long after the dawn broke: \emph{What
lies beneath the surface of our perfect life?}

Here is the continuation of the scene:

The night air was heavy with the scent of blooming flowers and fresh-cut
grass, a stark contrast to the turmoil brewing within Emma. She slept
fitfully, her dreams a jumbled mix of fragmented images and unsettling
emotions. Liam's presence beside her was comforting, yet even in
slumber, she felt an inexplicable sense of detachment.

As the first light of dawn crept into their bedroom, Emma stirred, her
mind foggy from the restless night. She lay there for a moment,
listening to Liam's gentle snores, before carefully extricating herself
from his embrace. The room was bathed in a soft, ethereal glow, as if
the morning sun was hesitant to disturb the tranquility of their
sanctuary.

Emma padded softly to the bathroom, her bare feet making barely a sound
on the chilled marble floor. As she splashed water on her face, her gaze
met its reflection in the mirror. The woman staring back at her appeared
serene, yet Emma sensed the turmoil simmering beneath the surface - a
maelstrom of emotions threatening to upend the carefully constructed
facade of her life.

With a deep breath, Emma began her morning routine, the familiar rituals
providing a sense of comfort and normalcy. As she dressed in the dimly
lit bedroom, Liam stirred, his voice husky with sleep.

"Morning, beautiful. What's the plan for today?" He sat up, rubbing the
remnants of slumber from his eyes, his gaze locking onto hers with an
intensity that made her heart skip a beat.

Emma forced a bright smile, her response automatic. "The usual -
sessions at the practice, and perhaps some paperwork in the evening.
You?"

Liam swung his legs over the side of the bed, his movements fluid as he
stood up. "A meeting with Alex to discuss the Smith account. Should be a
lucrative deal if we play our cards right." His eyes sparkled with
anticipation, yet Emma detected a flicker of something more - a hint of
tension that seemed out of place in their tranquil morning routine.

As they headed downstairs, the aroma of freshly brewed coffee enveloped
them, a warm welcome to the new day. Emma felt a sense of gratitude
towards Liam for this small gesture, yet the feeling was short-lived.
Her thoughts drifted back to the mysterious phone call, and the
unsettling sensation that had been growing since last night.

"Liam, can I ask you something?" Emma's voice was casual, yet laced with
a hint of curiosity, as they sat down to enjoy their coffee in the
sun-drenched kitchen.

"Of course, my love. What's on your mind?" Liam's response was
immediate, his eyes locking onto hers with an air of openness.

Emma hesitated, unsure how to articulate her concerns without sounding
paranoid. "It's just...I had that strange phone call last night, and I
couldn't shake off the feeling that something's off. Is everything
alright? With us, with your business -"

Liam's expression transformed, his features softening as he reached out
to cover her hand with his. "Everything's fine, Emma. You're just
stressed from work. The phone call was probably a wrong number, and my
business dealings are all above board. Trust me, I'd never do anything
to jeopardize our life together."

His words were reassuring, yet Emma sensed a faint undercurrent of
evasiveness. She pushed the thought aside, attributing her misgivings to
her own paranoia. As they finished their coffee, the morning sunlight
streaming through the window seemed to highlight the cracks in her
perception - tiny fissures that threatened to spread, undermining the
very foundations of her perfect life.

With a sense of trepidation, Emma rose from her chair, her movements
mirroring Liam's as they prepared to face the day ahead. The silence
between them was comfortable, yet Emma couldn't shake off the feeling
that she was walking on thin ice - one wrong step could shatter the
fragile equilibrium of their relationship, plunging her into an abyss of
uncertainty.

Here is the final chunk of the chapter, concluding the narrative
cohesively and meaningfully:

As they stood at the threshold of their home, the warm sunlight casting
long shadows behind them, Emma felt a sense of trepidation wash over
her. She glanced up at Liam, his profile strong and reassuring, yet her
mind lingered on the unspoken words, the hidden fears, and the
mysterious phone call that had set this chain of events in motion.

"I love you," Liam said, his voice low and sincere, as he leaned in to
brush a gentle kiss against her cheek. The touch sent a shiver down her
spine, but Emma's response was muted, her heart heavy with the weight of
unasked questions.

"I love you too," she replied, her voice barely above a whisper, as she
forced a smile onto her lips. The gesture felt like a betrayal, a facade
masking the turmoil brewing within her.

As they parted ways, Liam heading towards his car and Emma watching him
go, she couldn't help but feel like she was standing at the edge of a
precipice, staring into an unknown future. The perfect life they had
built together seemed to be teetering on the brink of collapse,
threatened by the very secrets and lies that had once held it together.

With a deep breath, Emma turned back towards her home, the door closing
behind her with a soft click. She leaned against it, her eyes drifting
shut as she let the silence envelop her. In that moment, she knew she
had a choice to make: to continue living the lie, or to confront the
truth head-on.

The decision would change her life forever.

As the stillness of the house wrapped around her, Emma's thoughts began
to unravel, each thread leading her closer to the realization that her
perfect life was, in fact, a carefully crafted illusion. The mysterious
phone call, Liam's evasive behavior, and her own growing sense of unease
all pointed to one inescapable truth: beneath the surface of their
flawless existence, a storm was brewing.

And Emma was about to face it head-on.


\chapter*{Chapter 3: Sarah's Concerns}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Chapter 3: Sarah's Concerns}
\textit{Emma's best friend, Sarah, expresses worries about Emma's relationship with Liam, revealing her own dark past.}

The aroma of freshly brewed coffee enveloped Emma as she settled into
the plush armchair across from Sarah at their favorite city center cafe.
The warm lighting and gentle hum of conversation from other patrons
created a cozy atmosphere, perfect for catching up with her best friend.
As they sipped their lattes, Sarah's expressive brown eyes locked onto
Emma's, filled with a deep-seated concern.

"Hey, Em, can I ask you something?" Sarah's voice was laced with
empathy, her tone gentle yet probing. "How's everything going with Liam?
You two seem...perfect, as always, but I've noticed you've been a bit
distant lately."

Emma's initial instinct was to brush off the concern with a reassuring
smile and a dismissive wave of her hand. It was a reflex honed from
years of maintaining a flawless facade, one that had become second
nature to her. "Oh, Sarah, everything's fine. Just busy with work and,
you know, life in general." She attempted to sound nonchalant, but the
words felt hollow even to her own ears.

Sarah's gaze didn't waver, her expression a testament to her ability to
see beyond Emma's carefully crafted mask. "Em, I've known you since
college. I can tell when something's off. Is everything alright between
you and Liam?" The question was posed with a sincerity that made Emma's
defenses wobble.

For a moment, Emma considered opening up, sharing the nagging doubts and
fears that had been accumulating in the recesses of her mind. But the
habit of secrecy, of protecting her perfect life at all costs, proved
too ingrained. Instead, she opted for a partial truth, hoping to placate
Sarah's concerns without revealing too much.

"Liam's just been...preoccupied with work lately. The Smith account is a
big deal for him, and I'm sure it'll all settle down once it's secured."
Emma's voice trailed off, the explanation feeling inadequate even as she
spoke the words.

Sarah's expression transformed, a mix of understanding and shared
experience etched on her face. "I know how that feels," she began, her
voice barely above a whisper. "Remember when I was with Jason - not the
current Jason, but the one from my past?" Emma nodded, recalling the
tumultuous relationship Sarah had endured years ago. "I thought it was
love, but in hindsight, it was just a masterfully crafted illusion. He
made me feel seen and loved, but only on his terms."

As Sarah spoke, her eyes clouded over, memories of a painful past
momentarily resurfacing. Emma reached out, covering Sarah's hand with
hers in a gesture of solidarity.

"I'm so sorry, Sarah. I had no idea it was that bad." Emma's voice was
filled with regret for not being more supportive back then.

Sarah's gaze refocused on the present, her expression determined.
"That's why I'm worried about you, Em. Liam might seem perfect, but
sometimes, it's the ones who appear flawless that hide the darkest
secrets. If something feels off, even if it's just a whisper in your
mind, don't ignore it. Trust me, I know."

The sincerity and depth of Sarah's concern struck a chord within Emma.
For the first time, she began to question whether her reluctance to
confront the issues in her relationship was truly about preserving
harmony or merely about maintaining the illusion of perfection. As they
sat there, hands clasped together, Emma felt the first tremors of doubt,
threatening to upend the carefully balanced scales of her life.

Here is the continuation of the scene:

The weight of Sarah's words settled upon Emma like a gentle mist,
seeping into the crevices of her mind and heart. She felt a shiver run
down her spine as she met Sarah's gaze, the concern etched on her
friend's face mirroring the growing unease within herself.

"I appreciate your worry, Sarah," Emma began, her voice measured as she
chose her words with care. "But I'm sure it's just my imagination
playing tricks on me. Liam loves me, and we're happy...aren't we?" The
question trailed off, a hint of uncertainty creeping into her tone.

Sarah's expression remained empathetic, yet resolute. "That's exactly
what I thought about Jason - that he loved me, that we were happy. But
